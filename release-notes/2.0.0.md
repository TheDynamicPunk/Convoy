**Convoy 2.0 is here, and brings torrent downloads!**

Paste a magnet link or open a .torrent file and Convoy handles it right alongside your browser downloads, YouTube videos and streams. Pick exactly the files you want, watch the pieces fill in, and decide how long you keep sharing. One app and one queue for everything you download, with a round of safety work and a few everyday upgrades on top.

### What's new

- **BitTorrent downloads.** Add a magnet link or open a .torrent file and Convoy downloads it like any other download, including pause and resume across quitting and reopening. Turn it on in Settings → Torrents, which installs the rqbit helper after checking it against a known checksum.
- **Choose your files.** A torrent with many files lets you pick exactly the ones you want before it starts, with a search box to narrow a long list.
- **See what's happening.** Expand a torrent's row for a live map of its pieces and details about its peers. After a relaunch it shows Checking while Convoy verifies what is already on your disk.
- **Seeding on your terms.** Stop when finished (the default), seed until a ratio, or seed for a set time. Settings → Torrents also has an upload speed limit, a fixed listening port if you need one, and switches for UPnP, local peer discovery and remembering DHT nodes.
- **Magnet links only if you want them.** Convoy never takes over magnet links by itself; Settings → Torrents has a button if you want it to.
- **Careful with your disk.** A torrent needs space for its whole size, so Convoy checks that before it starts. Adding the same torrent twice is recognised even if one came from a magnet link and the other from a file.
- **Select several downloads at once.** Labelled Select All, Deselect All and Delete buttons appear in the header while you are selecting.
- **Clear All Completed.** Removes finished downloads from the list and keeps their files. Delete All Completed still asks about the files.

### Improved

- **Helpers stay current.** Keep helpers up to date now covers the BitTorrent helper too, and new versions of it arrive with Convoy updates.
- **Honest progress after a force-quit.** An interrupted YouTube download now reopens showing what has really been saved, instead of jumping ahead when you resume.
- **Settings save when you click away.** Clicking outside a text field now saves what you typed, on every Settings tab.

### Fixed

- **Helpers kept running after Convoy quit or crashed.** A yt-dlp download could carry on in the background, and the YouTube bot-check helper stayed up. They now stop with Convoy, and Convoy cleans up any a previous run left behind.
- **A yt-dlp crash looked like a cancel.** It now shows as a failed download, with the error.
- **Unexpected quits.** Convoy and its browser helper no longer risk quitting when a connection closes at the wrong moment.

### Security

- **Safer redirects.** When a link sends you to another site, Convoy no longer passes along your cookies, logins or other site-specific headers, refuses redirects to anything that isn't a web address, and drops the referring page when a redirect goes from HTTPS to plain HTTP.
- **Stricter stream reading.** Video stream playlists and keys are size-limited, and malformed or oversized ones are rejected.
- **Tighter helper connections.** The YouTube bot-check helper only accepts connections from your own Mac, and a download can no longer ask for an unreasonable number of parallel connections.
